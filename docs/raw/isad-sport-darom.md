# Sport Darom (ספורט דרום)

Source: https://isad.org.il/clubs/ספורט-דרום/

## Organization Description

Sport Darom is a collaborative project of:
- Spivak Rehabilitation Center
- Ilan Association
- Israeli Paralympic Sports Union

**Mission:** Encourage and empower children, adolescents, and adults with physical disabilities or visual impairments in Be'er Sheva and the South. Provide equal opportunities for participants to develop athletic skills and represent Israel internationally, including at Paralympic Games.

## Sports Programs Offered (8 disciplines)

1. **Athletics** (אתלטיקה) - running, jumping, shot put, javelin
2. **Wheelchair Basketball** (כדורסל כיסאות גלגלים)
3. **Goalball** (גולבול) - for visually impaired athletes
4. **Swimming** (שחייה) - all levels
5. **Boccia** (בוצ'ה) - precision sport for physical disabilities
6. **Handcycles** (אופניים ידניים)
7. **Judo** (ג'ודו) - for visually impaired only
8. **Archery** (קשתות)

Programs accommodate participants aged 6+ with various physical and visual disabilities.
