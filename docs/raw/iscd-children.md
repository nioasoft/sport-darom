# ISCD Children and Youth Program (תוכנית ילדים ונוער)

Source: https://iscd.com/תוכנית-ילדים-ונוער/

## Main Program Overview

The Israel Sport Center for the Disabled (ISCD) invites children and teens with congenital or acquired disabilities to join a varied, professional, and enjoyable sport program led by qualified coaches. Activities accommodate ages 6+ in a supportive environment with individual adaptation.

## Sports & Activities Offered

The program includes:
- Wheelchair basketball
- Wheelchair tennis and table tennis
- Swimming (recreational and competitive)
- Boccia (adapted ball sport for cerebral palsy)
- Football, archery, and martial arts
- Gym training and adaptive cycling
- Wheelchair rugby

**Schedule:** Sundays and Wednesdays, 3:00 PM–6:00 PM

## Advanced Track: "Olympiakids"

For athletes with competitive potential, the center offers specialized training including:
- Professional coaching
- Strength conditioning
- Training camps
- Participation in national/international competitions

Sports in the advanced track:
- Swimming
- Wheelchair basketball
- Table tennis
- Field tennis
- Football
- Archery
- Boccia
