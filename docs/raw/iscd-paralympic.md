# Paralympic Sports at ISCD (ספורט פראלימפי)

Source: https://iscd.com/ספורט-פראלימפי-פאראלימפי/

## Overview

The center views Paralympic sport as a significant tool for:
- Personal development
- Strengthening self-confidence
- Fostering discipline

Athletes are encouraged to pursue excellence in competitive disciplines locally and internationally.

## Sports Offered

The center actively provides training in seven competitive sports:
1. Wheelchair basketball
2. Table tennis
3. Wheelchair tennis
4. Swimming
5. Boccia
6. Rugby
7. Archery

## Training & Support

The facility provides:
- Personalized coaching programs
- Experienced coaching staff
- Rehabilitation services
- Nutritional services
- Competitive frameworks at local and international levels

## Notable Achievements

Athletes from the center have represented Israel at major competitions:
- European Championships
- World Championships
- Paralympic Games (2020 Tokyo, 2024 Paris)
