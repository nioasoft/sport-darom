# Sports for People with Disabilities in Southern Israel (ספורט נכים בדרום)

Source: https://iscd.com/ספורט-נכים-בדרום/

## Overview

"Para Sport South" is a collaborative initiative of:
- Eilan Sports Center for the Disabled (Spivak), Ramat Gan
- Eilan Association
- Israeli Federation for Sports of the Disabled

**Mission:** Empower children, youth, and adults with physical disabilities or visual impairments in southern Israel by providing equal opportunities for personal development and competitive athletic representation.

## Available Sports (8 disciplines)

1. Wheelchair basketball (כדורסל כיסאות גלגלים)
2. Goalball (גולבול)
3. Hand cycling (אופניים ידניים)
4. Archery (קשתות)
5. Swimming (שחייה)
6. Athletics (אתלטיקה)
7. Judo (ג'ודו)
8. Boccia (בוצ'ה)
